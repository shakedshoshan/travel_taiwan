## Attribution (required)
- CC BY and CC BY-SA: show visible credit near the image or on a /credits page: use the `attribution` string from credits.json (title, author, licence + link, source).
- CC BY-SA: if you modify the image (crop/filter/compose), the modified image must be released under CC BY-SA too.
- CC0/PD: no credit legally required; credit kept anyway.
- match = "representative": a generic photo of the dish/type/city, NOT the specific restaurant/venue. Do not caption it as that exact place.
- Photos may show people/buildings incidentally; avoid using them in ads implying endorsement.
